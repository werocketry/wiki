# Contributing to the WE Rocketry Wiki

Welcome! This wiki is a collaborative knowledge base for the Western Engineering Rocketry Team. It’s designed to be easy for all members—regardless of technical background—to use, update, and improve. All contributors are welcome!

## What is this wiki?

This site contains our team’s history, procedures, technical documentation, and more. It’s meant to help everyone learn, share, and keep our knowledge organized.

## How to Use the Wiki

- **Browse:** Use the sidebar to navigate between pages like History, Procedures, and Contributing.
- **Search:** Use the search bar (top right or sidebar) to quickly find topics or keywords.
- **Download:** Some pages offer download buttons for files or resources.

## How to Contribute

You don’t need to be a tech expert! Anyone can help keep our wiki up to date:

### Editing Existing Pages

1. Click the “Edit” button (usually at the top or in the sidebar).
2. Make your changes in the online editor (Markdown format).
3. Save or submit your edits. Changes may be reviewed before publishing.

### Adding New Pages

1. Use the “Add Page” or “New Page” button (if available).
2. Give your page a clear title and write your content in Markdown.
3. Link your new page from relevant sections or the sidebar.

### Suggesting Changes

If you’re not comfortable editing directly, you can:

- Contact a team lead or wiki maintainer.
- Open an issue or discussion (if enabled).

## Contribution Guidelines

- **Be clear:** Write simply so everyone can understand.
- **Be accurate:** Double-check facts, procedures, and instructions.
- **Be respectful:** Keep content professional and inclusive.
- **Cite sources:** Reference documents, standards, or external links when possible.
- **Organize:** Use headings, lists, and links to keep pages tidy.

## Getting Help

If you’re stuck or unsure how to contribute:

- Ask a team member for help.
- Check the README for more info.
- Reach out via the contact links in the sidebar.

Thank you for helping us build and improve the WE Rocketry Wiki!
